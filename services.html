International

Apostille Certification

Under the Hague Apostille Convention, an apostille replaces the old, slow embassy legalization process — making your document legally valid in over 120 member countries with a single, unified stamp. We handle the entire chain, from document review to final apostille certificate.

  • Degrees & academic transcripts
  • Birth & marriage certificates
  • Corporate & commercial documents
  • 120+ Hague member countries
  • 24–72 hour turnaround
  • Government fees included

Attestation

Embassy & MEA Attestation

For documents heading to non-Hague countries, an apostille isn't enough — you need full-chain attestation from the notary to the MEA and finally the destination embassy. We handle every step in-house, so you never have to visit a single government office yourself.

  • MEA (Ministry of External Affairs)
  • Embassy & consulate attestation
  • Educational & personal documents
  • Commercial & corporate papers
  • Visa, work & study applications
  • 3–7 working day turnaround
